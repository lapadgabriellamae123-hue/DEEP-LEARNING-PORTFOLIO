This portfolio currently contains my lab notebooks for the Deep Learning course, covering forward/backward propagation fundamentals and building up to full PyTorch models:

  • Lab 2 - Forward Pass and Error

  • Lab 4 - PyTorch Linear Regression
  • Lab 5 - PyTorch Tensor Basics

Lab

Topic

Lab 2

Manual forward pass through a small neural network and computing the error, by hand and in NumPy

Lab 3

Manual forward and backward propagation (backprop from scratch) with a ReLU network

Lab 4

Training a linear regression model end-to-end in PyTorch (MSELoss, SGD, mini-batches)

Lab 5

Core PyTorch tensor operations — reshaping, dtypes, indexing, matrix multiplication

Lab 6

Converting a CNN architecture diagram into a working PyTorch nn.Module
